Codomax Digital Growth Campaign

30-Day Digital Marketing Internship Project

Project Overview

This project is a comprehensive 360° Digital Growth Campaign developed as part of my Digital Marketing & Growth Internship at Codomax Digital Solutions.

The campaign focuses on improving internship visibility, reaching students, increasing brand awareness, and creating opportunities to drive student registrations.

Project Objectives

Improve organic search visibility through SEO.

Identify high-intent keywords related to internships and technical training.

Develop social media marketing strategies to reach students.

Research backlink and directory submission opportunities.

Identify potential influencers and college partnerships.

Analyze competitors and identify growth opportunities.

Evaluate the internship landing page for conversion optimization.

Modules & Deliverables

Module 01 – SEO & Keyword Research

Researched high-intent internship-related keywords.

Prepared keyword research documentation.

Developed SEO-focused content and blog ideas.

Module 02 – Backlink Building

Researched 20+ backlink opportunities.

Prepared 10 outreach message templates.

Created a backlink tracking sheet.

Module 03 – Directory & Business Listings

Researched 30 relevant directories.

Prepared business listing and promotional research.

Organized directory submission opportunities.

Module 04 – Social Media Marketing

Created a 7-day social media content roadmap.

Developed 7 social media creatives.

Prepared captions and calls to action.

Drafted 3 LinkedIn promotional posts.

Module 05 – Influencer & Partnership Research

Identified 20+ influencer and partnership prospects.

Prepared collaboration messages.

Developed a college partnership proposal.

Created an outreach tracking sheet.

Module 06 – Competitor Analysis

Analyzed four internship and student opportunity platforms:

Internshala

AICTE National Internship Portal

Unstop

LetsIntern

Evaluated their marketing strategies, positioning, content and promotional opportunities.

Module 07 – Growth Campaign & Conversion Optimization

Reviewed internship landing page conversion opportunities.

Prepared SEO and promotional recommendations.

Organized campaign documentation.

Developed a final 360° digital growth campaign summary.
