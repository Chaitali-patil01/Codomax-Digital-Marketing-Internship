Personalization Checklist

Before sending any message:

Use the creator's correct name.

Mention one relevant post or topic from their profile.

Explain why their audience may benefit.

Include the official internship link.

Keep the message short and professional.

Avoid making unverified claims about stipends, certificates, placements or job guarantees.

Send messages only through an authorized company account.
