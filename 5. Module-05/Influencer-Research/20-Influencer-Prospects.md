Initial Evaluation Criteria

Before contacting any creator, review:

Whether they regularly publish relevant content.

Whether their audience includes college students or fresh graduates.

Whether their recent posts receive genuine comments and interactions.

Whether they are active on LinkedIn or Instagram.

Whether their profile provides a professional contact or collaboration option.

Whether their content and audience are suitable for internship promotion.

Priority Groups

High relevance: Coding educators, programming creators, data science educators and student career mentors.

Medium relevance: General technology creators and entrepreneurship educators.

Additional research required: Verify current activity, audience suitability, profile links, engagement and collaboration availability before outreach.

Important Research Note

This is an initial prospect list, not a verified contact database. Follower counts, contact details and engagement figures have not been assumed. No creator has been contacted yet.

The final shortlist should prioritize relevant student audiences and genuine engagement rather than follower count alone.
