4. Important Submission Considerations

Before submitting an article:

Read the website's latest guest post guidelines.

Check whether submissions are free or paid.

Confirm that the proposed topic matches the website's audience.

Prepare original, useful and well-researched content.

Follow the required word count and formatting.

Avoid promotional content unless explicitly permitted.

Wait for editorial approval before assuming an article will be published.

Important: A website accepting guest posts does not guarantee that Codomax's article will be approved. Some websites have strict editorial requirements, and some may charge for sponsored placements.
