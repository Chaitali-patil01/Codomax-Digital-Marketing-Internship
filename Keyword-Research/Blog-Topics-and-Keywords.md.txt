4. Target Audience

The target audience includes:

College students looking for practical experience.

Fresh graduates searching for their first opportunity.

Computer science and IT students interested in technical careers.

Students interested in digital marketing and online learning.

Beginners looking for professional certifications.

5. Recommendations

Prioritize long-tail keywords because they describe specific student needs.

Create informative blogs around career roadmaps and internship preparation.

Use relevant keywords naturally in headings, titles and descriptions.

Add links to the Codomax internship page where relevant.

Include clear calls to action encouraging interested students to explore internship opportunities.

Conclusion

The keyword research identifies 12 potential blog topics and four major keyword clusters. These topics can help Codomax create relevant content for students interested in internships, technical careers and professional development.
