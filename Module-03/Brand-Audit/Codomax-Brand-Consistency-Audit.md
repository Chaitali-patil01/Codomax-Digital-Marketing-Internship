3. Public Online Presence Review

A. Official Website

Website: https://codomaxdigital.in/

Status: Reviewed.

The official website describes Codomax as a technology and digital solutions company.

Recommended action:

Keep the company name consistent.

Use the official website as the main company URL.

Use the internship page for internship promotions.

Maintain consistent service descriptions.

B. LinkedIn Company Profile

Website: https://www.linkedin.com/company/codomaxdigital/

Status: Public profile identified.

Observations:

Company name appears as Codomax Digital Solutions.

Industry is shown as IT Services and IT Consulting.

The website field currently points to the internship page.

The About section contains a shorter description focused on graphic design and web development.

Recommended improvements:

Review whether the website field should point to the main official website.

Update the About section to reflect the full range of services described on the official website.

Verify company size, headquarters and other profile information with the company administrator.

Recommended action:

Review the company description.

Verify company location, industry and website.

Check whether the profile can be claimed or edited by an authorized representative.

5. Recommendations

Use the same authorized company name across all platforms.

Keep the official website URL consistent.

Use the correct internship landing page when promoting internship opportunities.

Maintain a consistent company description.

Use approved logos and brand images.

Verify company contact information before publishing.

Review existing directory listings periodically.

Remove or correct outdated information only with authorization.

6. Important Notes

This audit is based on publicly accessible information reviewed during the research.

It does not confirm ownership or administrative access to the identified profiles.

No company profile changes have been made as part of this audit.

Further verification and updates require authorization from Codomax Digital Solutions.

7. Conclusion

The brand consistency audit identified opportunities to improve consistency between the official website, LinkedIn company profile and external company directories.

Maintaining accurate company information, consistent descriptions and correct website links can strengthen online brand identity and credibility.
