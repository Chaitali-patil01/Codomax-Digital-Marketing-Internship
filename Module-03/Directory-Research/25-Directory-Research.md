4. Evaluation Criteria

Each platform should be evaluated using the following criteria:

Relevance to Codomax Digital Solutions.

Audience suitability.

Availability of company or employer registration.

Free or paid listing options.

Verification requirements.

Website quality and credibility.

Availability of an external website link.

Submission guidelines and approval process.

6. Important Verification Notes

These 30 platforms are research prospects, not confirmed listings.

Registration, eligibility and publication rules must be checked before submitting company information.

Some platforms may require payment, business verification or an established company profile.

Startup India recognition is subject to official eligibility criteria.

Product Hunt and G2 are not general-purpose company directories.

Internship advertisements must contain accurate role details, duration, eligibility and stipend information.

Do not publish listings without authorization from Codomax.

Do not create duplicate or misleading business profiles.

7. Conclusion

This research identifies 30 potential platforms across business directories, startup communities and student opportunity portals.

The next step is to evaluate suitable platforms, confirm company authorization and create accurate Codomax listings where eligible.
